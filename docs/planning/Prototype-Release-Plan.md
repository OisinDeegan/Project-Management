# Fix My Campus – Prototype Release Plan

**Status:** Hypothetical / Prototype Plan  
**Project:** Fix My Campus  
**Release Target:** End of Semester Week 12  
**Sprint Duration:** 1 Week  
**Estimated Team Velocity:** 10 Story Points per Sprint

## 1. Overview

This is an initial prototype release plan for Fix My Campus. It demonstrates how we could organise development into manageable one-week sprints.

The main goal is to produce a working prototype that allows students to report campus issues, track their progress and allow maintenance or IT staff to manage submitted reports.

This plan is hypothetical and will be reviewed by the team before development begins. The story points, sprint allocations and priorities may change based on our actual Jira backlog and available resources.

## 2. Planning Approach

We have used a release-date approach for this prototype because the deadline is fixed at the end of Week 12.

For this example, we assume four development sprints beginning in Week 9.

**Estimated velocity:** 10 story points per sprint.

**Total capacity:** 4 sprints × 10 points = 40 story points.

We have prioritised the essential functionality to ensure that the prototype remains manageable.

## 4. Proposed Release Scope

### Included in the Prototype

- Submit a report about a campus issue.
- Select the issue location and category.
- Store submitted reports.
- Receive a reference number for anonymous tracking.
- Check the status of a submitted report.
- Provide a dashboard for authorised staff.
- Allow staff to update issue progress.
- Allow staff to filter and organise reports.

### Not Included in This Release

- Student-to-staff messaging.
- Push notifications.
- Advanced analytics and reports.
- Integration with existing ATU systems.
- Full mobile application.

These additional features may be considered for future releases.

## 7. Risks and Limitations

The main risks are limited development time, technical difficulties, team availability and delays during testing.

To help manage these risks, the team will prioritise core reporting and tracking features before introducing additional functionality.
