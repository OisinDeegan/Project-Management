Definition of Ready (DoR) - Fix My Campus
A user story is considered ready for development when:
	• The user story is clearly written and easy to understand.
	• The story has acceptance criteria explaining what it must achieve.
	• The story is linked to the correct epic in Jira.
	• The story has been prioritised by the Product Owner.
	• The story has been estimated using story points.
	• Any dependencies or technical requirements have been identified.
	• The team has enough information to begin development.
	• The story is small enough to complete within one sprint.
	• The team agrees that the story is ready for development.
	• Tools have been set up, testing requirements for each User Story has been clarified
Purpose: To ensure that the team understands what needs to be developed before starting work on a user story.
